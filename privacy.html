Privacy Policy

MicroNothing Clock for Android · Last updated: October 28, 2026

01. Overview & Core Philosophy

MicroNothing Clock is built with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. We believe your personal schedule, to-dos, and ambient clock data belong strictly to you.

The app does not operate remote user tracking servers, does not maintain user accounts on proprietary databases, does not sell or share personal data with third parties, and does not serve behavioral advertisements.

02. Google Tasks API Data & Limited Use Disclosure

MicroNothing Clock provides an optional feature allowing users to connect their Google Account to display upcoming and due tasks directly on their home screen widget.

  • Scope Requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks.readonly
  • Purpose of Access: The app queries your Google Tasks solely to display the title, due date, and completion status of your upcoming to-dos within the widget interface (Row 3 and Row 4).
  • Local Storage: Retrieved task titles and due dates are cached locally on your device in secure, sandboxed application storage (SharedPreferences) to allow instant, offline widget rendering without unnecessary battery or network consumption.
  • Zero External Transmission: Your Google Tasks data is never uploaded, transferred, or backed up to any developer-owned or third-party servers. All API requests travel directly between your device and Google's official endpoints (tasks.googleapis.com).
  • Revocation: You can disconnect Google Tasks at any time from the app's Calendar & Agenda settings, which immediately clears all cached tasks and signs out your account.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: MicroNothing Clock's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

03. Device Calendar Data

If you grant the standard Android READ_CALENDAR permission, MicroNothing Clock reads your device's local calendar database (CalendarContract) to detect upcoming events today and calculate start time countdowns.

This calendar data is read entirely on your physical device, rendered directly to the widget canvas, and is never transmitted over the internet or collected externally.

04. Location & Weather Information

When Live Weather is enabled, the app requests coarse/approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to determine your local latitude and longitude.

These coordinates are used solely to query current temperature and weather conditions from open weather endpoints (e.g. Open-Meteo). Location coordinates are never tracked, stored in location histories, or shared with advertisers.

05. System Permissions

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to wake the device at the exact minute boundary (:00 seconds) to guarantee the widget digital clock remains perfectly synchronized with system time.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Restores widget alarms and schedules immediately after device reboot.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used exclusively to fetch weather forecasts and communicate directly with Google Tasks APIs upon user authorization.
