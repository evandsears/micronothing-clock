Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: Getting "Item not found" or "App not available"?

Make sure you are logged into the Google Play Store with the exact same Google email you used to join the Google Group. If you just joined, wait 2–5 minutes for Google's permissions to propagate, or open the link in a private/incognito tab signed into that account.

Q: How do I report a bug or suggest widget styles?

You can post directly in the Google Group discussion board or use the "Developer feedback" button in the Google Play Store testing listing.
