## 3. Full Description (3,703 / 4,000 Characters — Copy & Paste Ready)

Bring the iconic technical micrographics clock widget to any Android home screen. MicroNothing (Micrographics: Clock Widgets) is a standalone home screen widget combining a minimalist digital clock widget, live weather widget, and Google Calendar & Google Tasks agenda widget into one glanceable display—with zero KWGT setup or custom launchers needed.

Inspired by blueprint grids and Nothing OS 5.0 micrographics typography, our widgets render at native high resolution on your Android launcher with exact :00 minute ticks and zero battery drain.

─── WHY CHOOSE THIS MICROGRAPHICS WIDGET? ───

• 100% Standalone Android Widgets: Pin resizable 4x2, 4x3, or compact 2x2 widgets directly to your home screen. No paid KWGT Pro keys or complex editors needed.
• 4-Row Agenda or 2-Row Compact Widget: Show the full 4-row readout (Time, Weather, Calendar & Countdown), or hide the agenda row for a clean 2-row clock and weather widget.
• Native Vector Precision: Pill badges, starburst timezone markers, thermometer bars, and speech bubbles render on a native canvas so your home screen widgets stay razor-sharp.

─── 6 ADAPTIVE WIDGET THEME PRESETS ───

1. Blueprint Grid: Classic slate architectural micrographics grid with crisp contrast.
2. Floating Glyph (Wallpaper-Adaptive): Transparent floating widget that samples your wallpaper and Material You palette, tinting glyphs and timezone badges to match your background with strict WCAG contrast protection.
3. Frosted Glass: Translucent glassmorphism widget card with adjustable opacity and border glow.
4. Material You: Dynamic Android 12+ system color integration that adapts when your wallpaper changes.
5. AMOLED Dark: Pure #000000 obsidian background built for OLED battery savings.
6. MicroNothing Red: Deep obsidian surface paired with signature #D71920 ruby red accents.

─── LIVE GOOGLE CALENDAR & GOOGLE TASKS WIDGET ───

• Real-Time Calendar Sync: Reads your on-device calendar to display your next meeting in uppercase monospace type, updating instantly when events change.
• Google Tasks Sync: Connect your Google Account to display due to-dos ("TASK DUE") alongside daily meetings.
• Finger Tally Countdowns: Hand-gesture icons count down minutes or hours to your next event, switching to "HAPPENING [NOW]" while active and resetting to open-day phrases ("YOUR DAY IS OPEN") when clear.

─── LIVE WEATHER WIDGET & FORECAST LAUNCHER ───

• 15 Weather Glyphs: Vector icons and speech bubbles for Sunny, Clear Sky, Clear Night, Cloudy, Rain, Thunder, Storm, Snow, Breezy, Haze, and Severe alerts.
• Thermometer Bar Gauge: Visual temperature scale in °F, °C, or Kelvin (K).
• Day & Night Solar Cycle: Switches day and night weather icons via Open-Meteo (zero tracking, no API keys needed).

─── 4 INTERACTIVE HOME SCREEN HOTSPOTS ───

• Tap Time & Date → Opens your system Clock & Alarms app.
• Tap Weather Row → Launches the full Google Weather forecast.
• Tap Event or Task → Opens your Calendar agenda to that event.
• Tap Widget Frame → Opens the MicroNothing Companion Studio.

─── VARIABLE TYPOGRAPHY & ZERO-DRIFT TICKS ───

• 5 Geist Mono Font Weights: Light (300) to Bold (700) weights with auto-scaling cradle brackets.
• Geometry Controls: Adjust card translucency (0%–100%), corner radius (0dp–40dp), 12h/24h time, and cursor accents.
• Instant Unlock & Doze-Proof Ticks: Instant screen-unlock refresh plus Unrestricted Battery Mode setup so OEM battery savers never freeze your clock widget.
• Privacy-First: On-device calendar queries with zero trackers, zero ads, and no subscriptions.

Upgrade your Android home screen with the cleanest minimalist micrographics clock widget, weather widget, and calendar widget on Google Play.
